## Cognitive Traces

I have some experience with electronics; However, when it comes to project pitching and inserting creative input, I can still hold some insecurity whilst exploring such fields of practice. 

The research question remained consistent throughout the process. As time passed, it became increasingly clear what the key elements of the project were and how the research interests connected. It remains important for me to understand and unpack electronics. Simultaneously, I have also recognised that my focus is on a low-tech utopia, and I intend to establish that clear path moving forward into future exercises.

## Moral Traces

There were moments during ideation where inserting biology and biomaterials felt impossible. Neither the timeframe nor the infrastructure of the process allowed for the incubation period required by the fields that define my research question. I felt powerless at certain stages of this process, feeling extremely restricted both on the making and production side of design and unable to follow up adequately on the electronics, which clearly needed additional support.

As a team, our roles were very clear, which did not constrain us from rotating tasks and adjusting flow according to the demands of each step. The logic of the mechanisms underwent considerable trial and error, which compromised time for assembly. Nevertheless, it demonstrated how important constant and open communication within the team is, allowing us to reroute and support each other's developments naturally.

My honest perspective on AI is that its input is only valuable if you do not know what you are looking for regarding a specific subject. I believe in maintaining scepticism regarding the interchange of knowledge. For example, descriptions and guidelines of electronic components were simply incorrect, as was circuit logic. Writing to AI models or consulting the datasheet of a standard component takes the same amount of time. AI is a valuable tool, although its use should not become an impulse. It is also useful for externalising thoughts, recognising patterns in extensive files, and translations in a foreign country.

## Technical / Process Traces 

Adding to what could have been an assumption, understanding electromagnetic components proved challenging, showing little difference between on and off states. Our design proposal and device are highly sensitive, not only to data reception but also regarding the components themselves. Moving between labs and tests led to the corrosion of each element within the device's development. As a proof of concept, the device shows promising features that could be easily updated and refined.

Soldering is something I find highly engaging; it takes me back to jewellery making. Poor execution of this step undermines the efforts of coding and logic, making it essential to define a timeline specifically for assembly. The viscerality of conductive electronics combined with bioactive film is exciting to witness. That visible connection is what I consider most valuable for my study on tissue engineering applied to textiles.
