## Cognitive Traces
The ideas presented were ambitious, nevertheless i believe we got a good frame of conclusions. The turning point of the project was when we reached a point where we had to lean into the hybrid state of the ptototype. Besides the first tests with leds being satisfatory the scaling of the schematic to other components, showed to be less reliable in respect to the final form of the deliverable. Assuming the terminology of s3x toy was also detrimental to the selection making of the logic of the electronic communication with the skin. Firstly, the PH was seen as the main trigger to a digital reaction when in fact: Touch, was essencial for this proof of concept. It later allowed to expand the universe by brainstorming positions, pattermaking and the use of lube recipes.

It was a project with a low screentime percentage, allowing us to think with our hands and have an artisanal approach to this hybrid artifact of eletronics and biomaterials. Intersecting our background with fashion design.

## Moral Traces

The contact to the skin was a common ground for the intentions of the project. The main topic to debrief was the application of electronics into biofabrication. Between performative visuals, soundscapes and even holograms, the study of conductivity of such surfaces became a strong asset of our project. Conceptually, each reference brought to the table was valuable to the route of the narrative. 

The connection between healing, witchcraft and bdsm, became intuitively clear once we aknowledge the mutual process of rebulding and strenghtning of self indentity, expression and sexuality. Alongside the different fronts of activism applied to pleasure and social emancipation. AI was mainly used for coding and documentation. Even if not hugely used, it allowing us to prioritize the making and the mounting.

## Technical / Process Traces 

The framework, as not very friendly to biofabrication, really pressured the developement of the samples to use for the prototype. Although we showcased a good balance and structure between active and dorment stages of each phase. We kickstarted the production with batches of options. While they dried, we focused on the electronics, pattern cutting and testing, coding and documentation. 

It was difficult to let the phisical aspects of it, in order to focus on the code and programming, might have avoided it until strictly necessary. But the code and circuit proved to be easily assembled, which allowed a good pace to later translate it into the presented prototype. Looking back, I would have prioritized more the calibration of the sensors, so that the demonstration displayed a more realistic view to the potential of the presented concepts.
